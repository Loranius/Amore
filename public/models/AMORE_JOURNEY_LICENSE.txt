Assets used by the «Наш шлях» constellation scene.

The former skybox (amore_journey_skybox.glb, «Billions stars Skybox HDRI
panorama» by Aliaksandr.melas, CC-BY-4.0) is no longer shipped: the sky is
now generated procedurally (ADR-0214).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
amore_journey_sun.glb
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Asset: Sun
Creator: Kasugay — https://sketchfab.com/Kasugay
Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/sun-318145fc2fd94890929179039da4355d
License: CC-BY-4.0
License URL: http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The source GLB is stored unchanged.

Its baked baseColor texture is used at runtime as a luminance source; the
per-event colour is applied through an emissive multiplier so the baked detail
survives instead of being tinted. Nothing in the file itself is altered.
