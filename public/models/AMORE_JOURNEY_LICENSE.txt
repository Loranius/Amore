Assets used by the «Наш шлях» constellation scene.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
amore_journey_skybox.glb
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Asset: Billions stars Skybox HDRI panorama
Creator: Aliaksandr.melas — https://sketchfab.com/alexandr.melas
Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/billions-stars-skybox-hdri-panorama-7e3e8f94810b44dd837bacad6c65b5e8
License: CC-BY-4.0
License URL: http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Modified from the original, as CC-BY-4.0 permits. Geometry, UVs, material
and node hierarchy are untouched; the single 2048x2048 emissive texture was
re-encoded from PNG (8 087 KiB) to WebP quality 80 (691 KiB) at the same
resolution, and the texture is referenced through EXT_texture_webp. The
container shrank from 8.12 MB to 0.71 MB. Downscaling was measured and
rejected: at 1536 px the point stars smear (star-pixel PSNR falls from
23.1 dB to 15.6 dB).

amore_journey_sun.glb
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Asset: Sun
Creator: Kasugay — https://sketchfab.com/Kasugay
Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/sun-318145fc2fd94890929179039da4355d
License: CC-BY-4.0
License URL: http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The source GLB is stored unchanged. Its baked baseColor texture is used at
runtime as a luminance source; the per-event colour is applied through an
emissive multiplier so the baked detail survives instead of being tinted.
