Assets used by the «Наш шлях» constellation scene.

amore_journey_skybox.glb
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Asset: Billions stars Skybox HDRI panorama
Creator: Aliaksandr.melas — https://sketchfab.com/alexandr.melas
Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/billions-stars-skybox-hdri-panorama-7e3e8f94810b44dd837bacad6c65b5e8
License: CC-BY-4.0
License URL: http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The source GLB is stored unchanged.

A WebP re-encode was tried and reverted after measurement. It cost nothing in
sharpness (mean gradient magnitude on the live screen: 4.114 vs 4.088 for PNG)
but WebP subsamples chroma 2:1, and this panorama is magnified about 3x on
screen -- the red and blue sparks of individual stars merged into flat purple.
Lossless WebP weighs 7.98 MB, so it wins nothing. Downscaling is worse still:
at 1536 px the point stars smear (star-pixel PSNR falls from 23.1 dB to
15.6 dB).

amore_journey_sun.glb
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Asset: Sun
Creator: Kasugay — https://sketchfab.com/Kasugay
Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/sun-318145fc2fd94890929179039da4355d
License: CC-BY-4.0
License URL: http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The source GLB is stored unchanged.

Its baked baseColor texture is used at runtime as a luminance source; the
per-event colour is applied through an emissive multiplier so the baked detail
survives instead of being tinted. Nothing in the file itself is altered.
