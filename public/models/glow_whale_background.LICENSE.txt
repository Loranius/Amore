Runtime derivative used by Amore:
- rebuilt as a very low-poly distant-background silhouette for mobile performance
- decimated to 88 vertices / 185 triangles
- baked broad diffuse/emissive appearance into vertex colors
- removed textures, rig, skinning and skeletal animation from the shipped copy
- keeps the recognizable whale silhouette while movement is animated by the reef scene code

The original uploaded GLB is the source asset. Attribution and source metadata are preserved here because the tiny runtime derivative intentionally strips nonessential GLB metadata.
