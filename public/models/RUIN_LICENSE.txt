This project uses an optimized derivative as the stage of the Amore crystal
world. The authored ruin — floor, pedestal, colonnade and scattered masonry —
remains the source of the visual asset.

Changes made for web use (scripts/models/compress-ruin.mjs):
- the authored `Gem` node was removed, because the couple's own crystal grows
  in that place;
- textures were downscaled by role (512 px for floor, pedestal and pillars;
  256 px for the small masonry debris) and re-encoded from PNG/JPEG to WebP;
- unreferenced materials, textures and accessors left by the removal were
  pruned.

Geometry, UVs, node structure and material assignments are otherwise unchanged.
