The 1K JPEG maps were transcoded to WebP for the mobile scene. The roughness
map uses lossy compression; the OpenGL normal map uses high-quality lossy
compression. The diffuse map is not shipped: the reef stone takes only relief
from this set, its colour comes from the theme (`reefStoneSurface.ts`). No
displacement map is shipped or sampled.
