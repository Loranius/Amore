# Wishlist bubble depth fix

Visual acceptance criteria for the mobile cloud:

- bright specular highlight remains visible on a light display;
- lower-right hemisphere is clearly darker than the upper-left;
- complete white rim separates the sphere from the page;
- contact shadow anchors every bubble below its circular shell;
- halo remains soft and does not replace the contact shadow;
- photos and cutouts stay above the lower inner shadow;
- touch interaction must not retain desktop hover styling.
