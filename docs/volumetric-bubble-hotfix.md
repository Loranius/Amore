# Volumetric bubble visual hotfix

Acceptance criteria:

- the white upper-left specular highlight is visible on pink photos;
- the lower-right inner shade creates a spherical lens effect;
- the outer rim remains visible around the whole image;
- the bubble casts a separate contact shadow on the page;
- the photo sits inside the sphere instead of visually replacing the sphere;
- touch devices keep the resting transform without sticky hover.
